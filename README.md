# Drive Time 
Drive Time is an app designed to log driving hours
for new drivers that need a specific number of hours
to receive their drivers' license. 

This app was created for drivers in Colorado who are
under 18 and need at least 40 daytime driving hours
and 10 nighttime driving hours to qualify for their
license. It is NOT affiliated with the Colorado DMV,
so you will ultimately need to log these hours on 
paper or in RoadReady.
